# TrackFlow Project Brief

## Business

TrackFlow is a last-mile delivery and warehouse management company founded in 2009. About 130 employees support operations in the United States and Spain from warehouses in Los Angeles and Zaragoza.

TrackFlow stores inventory, picks and packs orders, coordinates eight carriers, delivers parcels, and handles returns for e-commerce brands.

## Objectives

- Give teams a shared view across both countries and warehouses.
- Reduce manual work in inventory, shipping, returns, customer service, and reporting.
- Improve operational visibility with clear, reliable interfaces and data.
- Build maintainable tools through the internal TrackFlow Tech unit.

## Problem We Solve

TrackFlow's operations depend on disconnected warehouse systems, spreadsheets, carrier portals, manual reports, and undocumented integrations. This causes delayed information, repetitive work, errors, and limited visibility. The project incrementally replaces those gaps with focused digital tools while preserving completed milestone work.
